Privacy policy

Trading Drive Archive is a personal archive tool. It copies only files the owner selects for archiving to the owner's Google Drive.

Google Drive access

The tool requests Google's per-file Drive permission. It uses file content and metadata to create archive folders, upload archives, identify existing copies, and verify that uploads match their sources.

Storage and sharing

The tool sends the archive files directly to Google Drive. It stores authorization credentials on the systems that run the uploader. Transfer logs may record file names, sizes, checksums, and results. The owner controls access to the servers and archive files. The tool does not sell or share Google user data with other recipients.

Retention and revocation

The owner controls how long to keep archives and may remove them from Google Drive. The owner can also revoke authorization through Google Account connections.
